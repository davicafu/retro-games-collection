# Leak (game 7)

Objective: seventh game of the roadmap, Pac-Man-inspired; introduces maze navigation and simple enemy AI on the fixed-tick runtime.
Branch: `feat/leak` (from `feat/botnet`) · Delivery: `ask-on-risk`, commits only when the user asks · Test runner: `go test ./...`

## Specs

Source requirements: README roadmap row `| 7 | Pac-Man-inspired | Maze navigation, simple enemy AI |` and the user's theme choice "Memory leak vs GC" (L2), whose description was: "Eres una fuga de memoria que se come los bloques libres de la RAM. Te persiguen recolectores de basura con distintas estrategias. Un power-up de 'malloc' te deja comértelos durante unos segundos." Everything marked *(assumption)* is a proposed design default, not a user requirement.

- **S1 — Theme.** "Eres una fuga de memoria que se come los bloques libres de la RAM. Te persiguen recolectores de basura con distintas estrategias." Original game: no names, characters, maze, sprites, sounds or enemy behaviours from Pac-Man.
- **S2 — Maze.** User (2026-11-12): "los laberintos son siempre iguales? se podrian cambiar o meter cosas diferentes no?", answered "la 1" (several hand-made maps that rotate by level). Three original maps, RAM, Heap and Stack, played in turn one per level (level n plays map (n-1) mod 3). *(assumption)* Each is left-right symmetric, 19×19 cells, with allocated blocks (walls), free bytes to eat, 4 `malloc` cells and a closed collector pool in the middle whose gate only collectors cross. Every corridor cell has at least two open neighbours (no dead ends) and every byte is reachable from the leak's start. Each cell is drawn 2 characters wide so the map looks square; walls are drawn as box-drawing outlines (the inside of solid blocks blank) and the box around the playfield stands for the map's outer ring.
- **S3 — Leak movement.** *(assumption)* The leak moves cell to cell at a fixed speed in the last direction pressed. A turn pressed early is kept and taken at the first cell where it is open; a reverse is taken at once; a wall ahead stops it. Horizontal moves are drawn in half-cell (one character) steps.
- **S4 — Collectors.** "recolectores de basura con distintas estrategias". *(assumption, which)* Three collectors leave the pool one after another. They never turn back except when a malloc starts or at a dead end, and at each cell take the open direction that brings them closest to their target by maze distance: the *Tracer* targets the leak's cell, the *Interceptor* the cell up to 4 cells ahead of the leak, the *Wanderer* turns at random unless the leak is within 4 cells, when it chases it. GC cycles (added in L5): collectors alternate a 6 s pause, heading for their own corner (Tracer top right, Interceptor top left, Wanderer bottom right), and a 15 s sweep, chasing; every life starts with a pause.
- **S5 — Lives and end.** *(assumption)* 3 lives. Meeting a collector (same cell, or crossing each other) costs a life: leak and collectors go back to their starts, eaten bytes stay eaten. After a start or a lost life, nothing moves until the player's first move. The round ends when lives run out.
- **S6 — Malloc.** "Un power-up de 'malloc' te deja comértelos durante unos segundos." *(assumption, how)* Eating a malloc makes the collectors outside the pool flee for a few seconds: they turn back, slow down and turn at random. Eating a fleeing collector scores 100, 200, 400 within one malloc and sends it back to the pool, from which it leaves again after a delay.
- **S7 — Levels and score.** *(assumption)* A byte scores 10 and a malloc 50. Eating every byte and malloc starts the next level on a refilled map: collectors get faster every two levels (6.5 cells/s up to 7.5, never as fast as the leak's 8), mallocs shorter (7 s down to 2 s) and the pool releases sooner (every 4 s down to 1 s). Levels are endless; the HUD shows level, score and lives.
- **S8 — Viability.** User on Botnet (2026-11-11): "tienen que ser viables". *(assumption: applied to Leak too)* An autopilot that only eats bytes it reaches before any collector clears the first 3 levels with its 3 lives in at least 27 of 30 seeded games, pinned by `TestAutopilotClearsTheFirstLevels` (`ViableLevels`, `ViableSeeds`).
- **S9 — Controls and flow.** *(assumption)* Arrows/WASD move. Pause, Game Over (score and level reached, Restart/Menu) and Q come from `internal/engine/session`. End title "COLLECTED".
- **S10 — Launcher.** *(assumption)* "Leak" appears in the launcher with its controls hint, and `play leak` starts it.
- **S11 — Engine.** Per AGENTS.md roadmap capability "navigation and light AI": maze distance fields (breadth-first search over passable cells) live in `internal/engine/grid`, since later games (the platformer's chasers) need the same navigation.
- **S12 — Determinism and timing.** Per AGENTS.md: injectable clock and RNG, scripted input, headless simulation tests; no sleep-based tests. 60 ticks per second like Drone, Breach and Botnet, with real-time speeds pinned by a test.
- **S13 — Colours.** User (2026-11-13): "en el leak (pacman), puedes usar colores para el mio (amarillo) y otros diferentes para los monstruos?" The leak is yellow; each collector kind has its own colour *(assumption, which)*: Tracer red, Interceptor magenta, Wanderer green; fleeing collectors blue, white while they blink. The map keeps the terminal's default colour. Colour is an engine capability: `render.Cell` carries a `Color` (bright ANSI palette, zero = default) and the shell sends frames with their escape codes; games that set no colour produce the same frames as before.
