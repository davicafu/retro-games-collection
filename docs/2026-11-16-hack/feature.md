# Hack (game 12)

Objective: twelfth and last game of the roadmap, "Hack the System": a hybrid of puzzle, arcade and narrative built on a simulated terminal, a network map and short arcade breaches.
Branch: `feat/hack`, to be created once the earlier games are committed (commits only when the user asks) · Delivery: `ask-on-risk` · Test runner: `go test ./...`

## Specs

Source requirements: README roadmap row `| 12 | Hack the System | Hybrid puzzle/arcade/narrative systems |`; the user's answer "me gustaria el 2. de fake terminal pero que no se centrase solo en terminal" (L2) to the option "Una terminal simulada donde escribes comandos (ls, cat, ssh…) para explorar un sistema ficticio, encontrar pistas y contraseñas; ciertos comandos lanzan minijuegos arcade contrarreloj. Más narrativo y de texto."; and "por ahora me vale" (L4) to the proposed mix (L3). Everything marked *(assumption)* is a design default, not a user requirement.

- **S1 — Terminal.** "Terminal (exploración y narrativa): escribes comandos (`ls`, `cat`, `scan`, `connect`, `decrypt`…) en un sistema ficticio; los ficheros y mensajes cuentan la historia y esconden pistas y contraseñas." *(assumption, how)* Commands: `help`, `ls`, `cat <file>`, `scan`, `connect <node>`, `decrypt <file> <key>`, `map`, `clear`. Typed text, backspace and enter work like a shell prompt; output scrolls.
- **S2 — Network map.** "Mapa de red (visual): `map` abre una vista gráfica de los nodos de la red: cuáles has comprometido, cuáles faltan y por dónde avanzar; te mueves por ella con las flechas." *(assumption, how)* Enter connects to the selected node when it is linked to one already owned; backspace returns to the terminal.
- **S3 — Breaches.** "Brechas (arcade): entrar en un nodo protegido lanza un minijuego corto y contrarreloj, de varios tipos: esquivar el ICE en una cuadrícula; alinear bits que caen para formar la clave; un puzzle de puertas lógicas." *(assumption, how)* Three kinds: ICE (reach the port across a grid while sentries chase), Sync (stop a sweeping signal inside the key window, three times), Gates (set the input switches so the circuit outputs 1). Each has a time limit; winning owns the node, losing raises the trace and drops the connection.
- **S4 — Trace.** "Rastreo: un medidor sube con los comandos fallidos y las brechas perdidas. Si se llena, te detectan y pierdes." *(assumption, how much)* Unknown or failed command +4%, wrong password or key +10%, lost breach +20%; at 100% the round ends, end title "RASTREO COMPLETO" (L8).
- **S5 — Goal and story.** "Pasarlo es llegar al servidor central y leer el último secreto." "Todo es original: historia, nombres y nodos inventados." Reading the final file on the central server wins, end title "ACCESO ROOT"; the summary shows the score, the trace, the hints and the time (L8).
- **S6 — Viability.** User on Botnet (2026-11-11): "tienen que ser viables". *(assumption: applied to Hack too)* An autopilot that types the solution and plays each breach at human pace wins at least 27 of 30 seeded games, pinned by a test.
- **S7 — Text input in the engine.** *(assumption, how)* The engine's input carries typed text as well as actions; a round that is taking text gets the letters as text only (Q and P do not quit or pause), while Esc still pauses and Ctrl+C still quits. Every other game keeps its keys exactly as before.
- **S8 — Launcher.** *(assumption)* "Hack" appears in the launcher with its controls hint, and `play hack` starts it. It is an original game, so no "-like" reference.
- **S10 — Hints.** "en hack estaria bien tener algun tipo de ayuda o pista y que cogerlas te reste puntos en la decision final" (L8). *(assumption, how)* `hint` shows, for the current stage of the run, a vague hint and then an explicit one; each new hint costs 100 points, repeating the last one is free and hints never raise the trace. The final score is 1000 less 100 per hint and 5 per point of trace, never below 0. The HUD shows the hints taken.
- **S11 — Spanish.** "me gustaria tambien que fuera en español en la medida de lo posible, los comandos y terminos habiutales en ingesl en ifnormatica no" (L8). Story, files, messages, help, HUD, map, breaches and end screen are in Spanish; commands (`help`, `ls`, `cat`, `scan`, `connect`, `decrypt`, `map`, `hint`, `clear`), machine names, breach names (ICE, SYNC, GATES) and common file names stay in English. *(assumption)* The launcher, shared by every game, stays in English.
- **S12 — Levels.** "me parece perfecto el hack como el nivel 1 o tutorial, podrias hacer dos mas donde se incrementara la dificultad tanto de las pruebas como de la logica" (L9). *(assumption, how)* Three levels, each a new network and story; level 1 is the current one. Reading a level's final file clears the screen and opens the next level; reading level 3's final file wins. The HUD shows the level.
- **S13 — Harder breaches.** "se incrementara la dificultad ... de las pruebas" (L9). *(assumption, how much)* Per level 1/2/3: ICE walls 16/20/22, sentries 3/4/5 moving 4/5/5.5 cells/s, 20/20/18 s (L10); Sync window 5/4/3 cells, 3/4/5 locks, cursor 20/30/30 cells/s, 20 s; Gates 5/6/7 switches, 30/25/25 s.
- **S14 — Harder logic.** "se incrementara la dificultad ... de la logica" (L9). *(assumption, how)* Level 2 adds machines that stay closed until their firewall machine is yours, and a password built from clues in two files. Level 3 adds a honeypot machine whose connection raises the trace by 30, warned about in a file, and an admin key split in fragments across machines that must be joined to decrypt.
- **S15 — Score per level.** *(assumption)* Trace and hints restart on each level; each finished level scores 1000 less 100 per hint and 5 per point of trace; the final score is the sum of the finished levels (a run caught by the trace keeps only those). The HUD and the summary show the level.
- **S16 — Viability of every level.** *(assumption)* The autopilot plays the three levels in a row and finishes level 3 in at least 27 of 30 seeded games.
- **S17 — Levels 4 and 5.** "haz un dos últimos niveles metiendo algo más nuevo de lógica en cada uno De ellos" (L11). *(assumption, how)* Level 4 adds active watching: while you stand on a watched machine the trace rises by 1 every second, until the level's `ids` machine is yours. Level 5 adds a lockout: the key machine's password must be deduced by ruling out candidates with clues from several files, and two wrong tries lock it for good, leaving only a breach to force it. Both levels keep the earlier mechanics and harder breaches; level 5's secret wins the game.
- **S18 — Target difficulty.** *(how, L12)* Levels 4 and 5 also make a lost breach add 50% of trace (20% before) and watching add 1% every 0.67 s and 0.5 s. "el cuarto debe pasar el autopilot solo el 50% y el 5 el 40%" (L11). The autopilot, starting fresh on each level, clears level 4 in about 50% and level 5 in about 40% of seeded games, pinned by a test (with a tolerance of a few points over the seeds); levels 1 to 3 keep at least 27 of 30.
- **S9 — Determinism and timing.** Per AGENTS.md: injectable clock and RNG, scripted input, headless simulation tests; no sleep-based tests. 60 ticks per second, with real-time limits pinned by a test.
